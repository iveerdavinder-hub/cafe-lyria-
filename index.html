Privacy & Policy

How The Hedgehog Cafe respects, secures, and protects your guest data.

🛡️ 1. Information We Collect

When booking a table or ordering in-café, we may collect your name, phone number, party size, and seating or dietary notes to provide seamless table hospitality and confirm your reservation.

Strict Guest Guarantee: We do not sell, rent, or trade guest contact information to advertising brokers, marketing agencies, or any external third parties.
🍽️ 2. How Your Information Is Used

Your details are used solely to send SMS confirmation receipts, prepare reserved seats at Sector 7-C, and coordinate kitchen food orders directly to your table.

💾 3. Local Storage & Security

Orders and reservation slips are processed securely in your browser session and transmitted to our verified on-site cafe dashboard for immediate kitchen fulfillment.

📍 4. Direct Inquiries & Data Removal

You may request removal of booking history anytime by visiting our counter at Inner Market, Sector 7-C, Chandigarh, or contacting our concierge at 0172 473 0478.

Terms & House Rules

Creating a serene, welcoming reading & artisanal cafe space for everyone.

📚 1. Library & Book Etiquette

Our library collection is freely accessible for all patrons. Please handle books with gentle care, avoid dog-earing pages, and return books to the shelves before departing.

⏰ 2. Reservation Holding Policy

Reserved tables are held for up to 15 minutes past the reserved time slot. If you are delayed, kindly contact us so we can maintain your designated table.

💻 3. Quiet Study & Remote Work

High-speed Wi-Fi and charging points are provided for your work and study sessions. We kindly ask guests to use headphones for calls and audio to preserve the tranquil atmosphere.
