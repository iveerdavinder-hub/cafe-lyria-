# Café Integral — 149 Elizabeth St, Nolita, NYC

Specialty single-origin Nicaraguan coffee house and kitchen in Nolita, New York. Founded in 2012 by César Vega.

### Key Features
- **Nicaraguan Single-Origin Coffee**: Dedicated micro-lot roasts, slow drip, pour-overs, and espresso.
- **House-Crafted Alt-Milks**: Almond-cashew-coconut and cashew-pepita milks made fresh in-house daily with dates.
- **Menu Highlights**: Signature Horchata Latte, Flat White, Matcha Fizz Iced, Cortado, Toasted Muesli, Chia & Tapioca Pudding, Olivia Muffin, and fresh Artisanal Croissants.
- **Table Reservation System**: Self-service booking with seating zones (Oak Slow Bar, Nolita Window Bench, Communal Wood Table, Elizabeth St Bistro).
- **Automated 1-Hour Free Cancellation**: Self-service 1-hour cancellation via SMS and email links.
- **Multi-Channel Confirmation**: SMS dispatch simulation, email receipt, and Google Calendar / `.ics` invite generation.
- **Direct Online Ordering**: Seamless integration with Google Online Ordering viewer.
- **Owner Admin Portal (`admin.html`)**: Passcode-protected live management dashboard (default passcode `1660` or `1490`), live audio chime, notification center, and floor plan management.
- **Local Business & SEO**: Schema.org JSON-LD structured data, Google reviews (4.5 ★ / 521 reviews), $1 - $10 price tier, LGBTQ+ friendly, women-owned identification.
