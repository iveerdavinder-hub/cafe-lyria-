Our Heritage & Mission

Elevating Nicaraguan coffee from farm to cup.

Café Integral was founded in 2012 by César Vega with a singular commitment: to showcase the exceptional, often underrepresented quality of Nicaraguan specialty coffee in New York City.

Vega drew from his family roots in Nicaragua and connections with smallholder farmers in Jinotega, Matagalpa, and Dipilto. By working directly with producers, Café Integral sources micro-lots roasted with careful nuance to honor the soil, altitude, and people behind every harvest.

Our Nolita home at 149 Elizabeth Street continues that mission daily with in-house plant-based milks, comforting breakfast food, and warm community hospitality.
