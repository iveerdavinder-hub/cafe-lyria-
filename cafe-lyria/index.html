About The Hedgehog Cafe

Where stories, great food and conversations meet.

Established in the heart of Sector 7-C Chandigarh, The Hedgehog Cafe was conceived with a simple philosophy: to build a warm, unhurried haven away from the hustle of the city.

Surrounded by warm mahogany wood paneling and walls lined with handpicked literature, visitors can pick any book from our collection to read during their meal, work with reliable Wi-Fi, or gather with friends over piping hot thin-crust pizzas and spinach ravioli.

We take pride in our polite hospitality, calm environment, and food prepared fresh to order using quality ingredients.
